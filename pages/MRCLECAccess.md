  <h1><b> How to access MRC LEC study data </b></h1>
  <p>  </p>
  
  <p>Information about how to access data from the studies hosted at the MRC Lifecourse Epidemiology Centre is provided below. For information about accessing data from other CLOSER Discovery studies, please see the <a href="/page/faqs/4#access-data">FAQs</a> or the data location links for the dataset you are interested in.</p>

<h2> <b> Health and Employment After Fifty Study</b></h2>
  <p>
    Bona fide researchers who want further information about the study, or who wish to apply for a collaborators’ agreement, should email Prof Elaine Dennison (emd@soton.ac.uk), Professor of Musculoskeletal Epidemiology and Honorary Consultant in Rheumatology within Medicine at the University of Southampton. 
  </p>


<h2> <b>Hertfordshire Cohort Study</b></h2>
  <p>
    Bona fide researchers who want further information about the study, or who wish to apply for a collaborators’ agreement, should email Professor Elaine Dennison (emd@soton.ac.uk), Professor of Musculoskeletal Epidemiology and Honorary Consultant in Rheumatology within Medicine at the University of Southampton. 
  </p>

<h2> <b>Southampton Women’s Survey</b></h2>
  <p>
   Bona fide researchers who want further information about the survey data that is available, or who wish to apply for a collaborators’ agreement, should contact members of the SWS leadership team: Sarah Crozier (src@mrc.soton.ac.uk) and Janis Baird (jb@mrc.soton.ac.uk). 
  </p>
